## Time

The farm moves in **ticks**. Your live farm runs at 10 ticks a second, all the time, whether or not you are
watching. Every action a bot takes costs a few ticks, and a bot can only start its next action when the last one
has finished. Questions, like looking at the soil or checking your coins, cost nothing.

## Land

The world is an endless grid of tiles. You own land in **plots** of 8 by 8 tiles. Your first plot covers
tiles `(0, 0)` to `(7, 7)`; the plot next to it on the east is plot `(1, 0)` and covers `(8, 0)` to `(15, 7)`.
Tile `x` grows to the east and `y` grows to the south.

The **barn** is tile `(0, 0)`. You can't farm it, and it's where bots drop off their harvest and where all
selling happens.

You can buy any plot that shares an edge with one you already own, with `buy_plot px, py`. The price is
150 × n², where n is the number of plots you own now: 150 for your second plot, 600 for the third, 1,350 for the
fourth. Bots can only walk on land you own.

## Bots

Each bot carries up to 20 units (30 after researching the satchel). A new bot appears on the barn and costs
80 × m², where m is the number of bots you own now. All bots run the same program, and `my_id()` tells each one
which bot it is.

## Crops

<CropTable detail />

A crop goes through stages 0 to 3 and is **ripe** at stage 3. Harvesting it before then gives nothing.
Berries and apples are **perennial**: after a harvest they stay planted and grow again. Every other crop
leaves the tile bare and untilled, ready to till and plant again.

**Fast growth.** When you plant, the crop checks the soil. If the moisture is at least the crop's *Water*
number and the nitrogen is at least its *Nitrogen* number, it grows at double speed. Otherwise it grows at normal
speed. The check happens once, at planting (and again each time a perennial regrows), so water first, then plant.

## Soil

Every tile has three numbers from 0 to 100. Look at them with `scan()` or the overlays in the game.

- **Moisture.** Starts at 50. Planting drinks some of it. `water` adds 30. Rain adds it back slowly.
- **Nitrogen.** Starts between 30 and 50, different on every tile. Most crops use it up when harvested.
  Clover adds 20. Compost adds 25.
- **Fatigue.** Starts at 0. Harvests raise it, and every point of fatigue cuts the harvest by 1%. A tile with no
  crop on it rests and recovers one point every 16 ticks. Clover and compost lower it faster.

## Weather

The sky is clear most of the time. Rain comes in spells and slowly waters every tile. **Frost** comes less
often, and kills any carrot or pumpkin that isn't ripe yet when it arrives. Frost also pauses their growth.
Wheat, clover, berries and apples don't mind it.

The weather is fixed in advance for every farm, so `forecast(500)` tells you exactly which rain and frost spells
start in the next 500 ticks.

## Selling and orders

At the barn, `deposit` moves everything a bot carries into barn stock, and `sell item, n` sells it at the
item's price for the day (the base price, until the [daily market](/docs/market/) opens on 8 December). `sell carrot, all` sells the whole stock.

Every so often a buyer posts an **order**: a crop, a quantity and a deadline. Filling it with `deliver`
pays twice the market price plus 10 coins. Orders get bigger as your farm does. `orders()` lists the open ones.

## Net worth

Your score is your **net worth**: your coins, plus everything you've ever spent on land, bots, buildings and
research, plus the market value of what's in the barn. Spending never lowers it, so buying a plot is never a
step back on the leaderboard.
