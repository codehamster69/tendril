## Install or play in the browser

Download the game for [Windows, macOS or Linux](/download/), or [play it in your browser](/play/). Both are the
same game. The desktop app keeps your farm on your computer and can sync it to the leaderboard; the browser
version keeps it in the browser you play in.

## What you start with

Every new farm gets one 8 by 8 plot of land, a barn in the corner at position `(0, 0)`, one bot standing on
the barn, no coins, and this program:

```sprout
# Start with carrots. Questions are free; actions cost ticks.
repeat forever:
    for each tile in tiles():
        go_to tile
        if scan().ripe:
            harvest
        if scan().empty:
            till
            plant carrot
        if carried() >= capacity():
            go_to barn
            deposit
            sell carrot, all
    go_to barn
    deposit
    sell carrot, all
    wait 100
```

Press **Run** and watch. The bot walks every tile, plants carrots, harvests the ripe ones, and sells them at
the barn. Coins start coming in after about a minute.

## The screen

- **The farm** on the left. Click a tile to inspect its soil. Overlays show moisture, nitrogen and fatigue.
- **The editor** on the right, with autocomplete and inline errors. The line each bot is running is marked as it runs.
- **The console** under the editor shows anything your program prints with `say`, plus runtime errors.
- **Run, Pause, Step** control the program. Breakpoints pause on a line so you can look at every bot's variables.
- **The top bar** shows the tick, your coins, your net worth and the weather.

## Make your first change

Carrots sell for 2 coins. Wheat sells for 3, yields twice as much, and survives frost, but you have to research
it first for 40 coins. Change the planting lines to this:

```sprout
        if scan().empty:
            till
            if unlocked(wheat):
                plant wheat
            else:
                plant carrot
```

And add a line near the bottom of the loop, next to the selling:

```sprout
    unlock wheat
    sell wheat, all
```

`unlock` is a farm action. It costs no time, and it simply does nothing until you can afford it. Press **Run**
again. Every bot restarts from the top of the new program, and the farm carries on where it was.
