A **seed packet** is a program a player shared. You can plant one in your editor, run it, change it, and share your own version.
Remixes link back to the packet they came from, so every packet has a family tree.

## Plant one

In the game, press **Start from a seed packet** above the editor, pick one and press **Plant this**. It loads into the editor.
If your editor already has code, the game asks first, and `Ctrl+Z` brings your code back.
On the site, open a packet at [/packets/](/packets/) and press **Plant it in the game**. In the terminal, run `tendril packet plant <id>`.

Planting works in Endless and Sandbox. While a daily or season is open it is switched off, so everyone solves the day on their own.

## Share one

Press **Share** above the editor (Endless or Sandbox), give it a name and a short note, and pick up to three tags.
The program must compile. You can share up to 10 packets a day. Names and notes cannot hold links, and the same program cannot be shared twice.
If you planted it from another packet and changed it, it is listed as a remix of that packet automatically.

**A program in play on an open daily or season cannot be shared until that challenge closes.** This keeps the day fair: nobody can
read a solution that is still being used. Programs from Endless and Sandbox can be shared any time.

## What the packet page shows

- The program, with colours, and a button to copy it.
- The **family tree** of remixes around it.
- The **best result**: the best verified score of a published program from a closed daily or season that matches the packet's
  program (spacing and comments ignored), with a link to its replay.

Your profile lists your packets and how many players planted them.

## Reports

Report a packet that does not belong. Three reports from accounts that are a week old and have a verified run hide it. A hidden packet disappears from the lists, its page and its author's profile. Reports never change anyone's score.
