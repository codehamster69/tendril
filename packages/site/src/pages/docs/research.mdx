## Research

A new farm can only plant carrots. Everything else is **research**, bought once with `unlock name` and kept
for good. Unlocking is a farm action: it costs no ticks, doesn't end the bot's turn, and returns `false` without
doing anything if you can't afford it yet. Research counts toward your net worth.

Orders only ask for crops you have researched.

## Buildings

A bot builds on the tile it stands on with `build hive` or `build composter`. The tile must be plain grass:
not the barn, not tilled, and without a crop. Building takes 4 ticks and costs coins on top of the research.
`clear` removes a building (or a crop, or tilled soil) for good, with no refund.

### Hives

A hive makes one jar of honey every {HONEY_INTERVAL} ticks and holds up to {HONEY_MAX}. A bot standing on the
hive collects it with `harvest`. Honey sells for 8 coins, more than any crop. `scan().honey` tells you how
many jars are waiting.

Hives also **pollinate**. When a ripe crop is harvested, every hive on the four tiles directly next to it
(north, south, east, west) adds 25% to the harvest. A crop surrounded by four hives yields double.

### Composters

Stand on a composter and `compost` turns every four crop units the bot is carrying into one unit of compost.
Compost can't be sold or deposited. Carry it to a tired tile and `fertilize` uses one unit to add 25 nitrogen
and take away 20 fatigue. That keeps your best tiles producing without resting them.
