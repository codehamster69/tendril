## What it does
- Cache headers (`withCacheHeaders`): anonymous, successful GETs of leaderboard, stats, daily, challenge, board, entry, replay get `public, s-maxage=N, stale-while-revalidate`. Any request with an Authorization or Cookie or Origin header, every error, and every private route stays `no-store`, so a viewer-specific answer is never shared. A new read route is uncached until added to `edgeSeconds`.
- Prune (`prune`, bounded per run, idempotent): closed challenges (2 days after closing) lose the log and source of every entry outside the top 100 of each medal (scores, ranks and mosaics stay); duel logs expire after 90 days; Endless chunks older than 14 days go, except each farm's newest; expired sessions, oauth states, desktop logins; attempt rows older than 2 days; net-worth samples older than 3 days (each farm keeps its newest sample at or before 24h ago, so growth stays right). Open challenges are never touched.
- Runs from the Vercel cron (04:17 UTC) AND lazily on the first request of each UTC day (claim in `arena_meta`, so only one instance runs it). The cron route needs `CRON_SECRET` set in Vercel; without it the route answers 401 and the lazy path still prunes.
- Guard (`refreshGuard`, re-measured at most every 10 minutes, stored in `arena_meta`): ratio = max(database size / `TENDRIL_STORAGE_BUDGET_BYTES` (default 4 GB), projected monthly rows written / `TENDRIL_WRITES_BUDGET_PER_MONTH` (default 8M)). Database size comes from page counts (no table scan). The write rate is sampled from the last 5 minutes of chunks and entries. Modes in order: 50% lean (new entries outside the top 100 are stored without a log), 70% tight (per-user attempt limits x0.25), 90% read-only (every POST except sign-in answers 503 with Retry-After). A mode releases 5 points below the level that engaged it.

## Projection: what the free tier carries (estimates, not measurements)
Rows written per action: stored entry about 3; verified Endless chunk about 4 (farm update, chunk, worth sample, attempt).
- Daily players only: 5 submissions a day is about 15 writes, so 8M a month carries roughly 17,000 daily players on writes.
- **Endless auto-sync is the real write cost.** At the 15 s sync, one player left running all day writes about 23,000 rows a day, 690,000 a month: about 11 always-on players fill the budget. A player online 2 hours a day costs about 58,000 a month, so roughly 140 of them.
- **Reads, board ranking.** `submitEntry` ranks the new entry on all three medals by scanning the challenge's entries, about 6N rows read for N entries so far. With 3 submissions per player, daily players P cost about 270 x P squared rows read a month, so the 500M read budget (400M used) is reached near 1,200 daily players. Cached board GETs are cheap; submissions are not.
- Storage is not the limit for a long time: a stored log is a few KB, pruning keeps only the top 100 per medal per closed day, and chunks are capped at 14 days.

## Recommendations (not done here, outside F2 scope)
1. Slow the Endless sync (60 s instead of 15 s), or write a worth sample at most every 10 minutes per farm. Either cuts writes 4x or 25%.
2. Make the post-submit ranking cheaper (rank only the medals that improved, using COUNT queries on the existing indexes instead of the window CTE). This moves the 1,200-player read ceiling up by an order of magnitude.
3. Re-check the Turso and Vercel numbers when billing emails or platform notices arrive; they are constants at the top of `housekeeping.ts` plus two env vars.
