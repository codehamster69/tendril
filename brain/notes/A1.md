Net worth: min 286, 5th lowest 290, median 310, max 352. Values below 290: 286, 286, 288, 288 (4 seeds), so par 290 is cleared on 96/100 (requirement: at least 95). 290 is the highest par that qualifies.

## Decisions
- Daily number 1 = 2026-12-01 (Season 0 launch day). Dates before it give 0 or negative.
- `parTick` is checked at tick 0, after every action and after every tick, and reports `world.tick` then; a sale applied at tick t counts as t. Final net worth is at `toTick` (actions at `toTick` are illegal, same as `replayChunk`).
- `replayWithPar` repeats `replayChunk`'s validation instead of wrapping it, since par must be checked between ticks. A test asserts identical hash/net worth.
- `farmMosaic` uses `scanAt` (settled, read-only), so frost-killed crops show as empty. Precedence: barn, hive, composter, crop, tilled, untilled.
- Calibration regression test is not in `sim/test` (sim cannot import lang); suggestion for a later task: add a 100-seed check to `packages/lang/test`.
