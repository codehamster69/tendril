# U8: seed catalogue

Branch off `claude/project-thread-ioqf7g`; PR targets it.

## Changed files
- New: `catalogue.ts` (pure data and rules of presentation, tested), `catalogue-view.ts` (overlay, badge, stamp), `catalogue-fx.ts`, `catalogue.css`, `catalogue.test.ts` (added to the client test list).
- Shared files, one-line or small removals: `main.ts` (import + `mountCatalogue`, after `mountStage`), `stage.ts` (menu entry "Seed catalogue" fires `tendril:open-catalogue`; "Progression" entry became "Achievements"), `index.html` (Research tab and deck removed), `progression.ts` / `progression.css` (old research tree and its rules removed).

## Decisions
- The sim has no prerequisites between unlocks, so every card says "Needs: nothing first". Vines show the price order (crops) and turn green once the earlier card is owned; they are decoration, not a rule.
- Research is only ever bought by the sim op `unlock`, which replays on the server. So "Add to code" appends `unlock <name>` to the editor (once) and closes the catalogue; the player presses Run. The stamp plays when a snapshot shows the bit newly set, which also fires the old toast. No rule, cost or order changed.
- Pictures are the existing `assets/thumbs` PNGs, bundled by Vite (about 100 KB). Hive, composter and satchel use the beehive, compost bin and crate pictures.
- Badge (menu button and menu entry): shows when a card is affordable and not yet seen; clears when the catalogue opens. "Seen" names are stored in `tendril.catalogue.seen.v1`.
- Keyboard: Ctrl+menu entry opens it, focus goes to search, Tab is trapped, arrows move between cards, Esc closes and returns focus to the menu button. Reduced motion removes glow, pulse and stamp animation.
- The overlay re-renders only when a card changes state, so focus survives coin ticks.
