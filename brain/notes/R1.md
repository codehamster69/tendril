## API
`rateContest({standings:[{id,place,rating,events}], weight, algoVersion}) -> [{id,performance,delta,rating}]`, `tierOf(rating) -> {key,name,floor,next}`, `TIERS`, `START_RATING`, `PLACEMENT_EVENTS`, `ALGO_VERSION` (=1), plus constants for every setting (`MIN_FIELD`, `DAILY_CAP`, `SEASON_CAP`, `SEASON_WEIGHT`, `PLACEMENT_MULTIPLIERS_PM`, `ESTABLISHED_FIELD_MIN`, ...).

## Decisions
- Elo curve: stored table `ELO_TABLE[d]`, d=0..2400, scale 1e6 (generated once by `scripts/gen-elo-table.mjs`, floats only there). Negative diffs use `1e6 - T[-d]`; beyond 2400 = 1 or 0. Engine is integer-only (one BigInt isqrt for the geometric mean).
- Expected place from counts per distinct rating value: F[p] precomputed for p=0..4000 (O(distinct ratings x 4001)), then O(1) per evaluation; binary search per player. 10,000 players well under 1 s (see test).
- Output sorted by (place, id), so it is input-order independent. Ratings clamped 0..4000. Own entry excluded from own expected place.
- Players outside the field (placement accounts once 30 established exist) are still rated, against the established field; their place = 1 + field members above (+ half of ties).
- Tie places: equal `place` values share the average (works with 1,2,2,4 style input).
- Field-size weight at 8 players is not given by the plan: chosen 0.3, linear to 1.0 at 30 (`MIN_FIELD_WEIGHT_PM`). Field size = number of players in the field (after the established-only rule). Field < 8 returns `[]` (unrated).
- Season cap (200, weight 1.5) is chosen when `weight >= 1.5`, else daily cap 150. Cap applies before zero-sum, so zero-sum can nudge a delta 1-2 points past a cap.
- Zero-sum: established players (events >= 5) sum to exactly 0; integer remainder given one point each in id order.
- Delta = trunc((performance - rating) x placement x fieldWeight x weight / 2), all in thousandths.
- `algoVersion` must equal `ALGO_VERSION` or it throws (recompute with a new version = new engine code).

## Caveats / for R9
- Drift is 0 from day 30 by construction (zero-sum). The established mean sits 55 above START_RATING because placement players' early gains are injected (placement multipliers are not zero-summed). One-off bootstrap in the sim; watch on the live ladder.
- Fake-farm leaks found: (1) Early ladder (days 1-5, fewer than 30 established): fakes' losses are redistributed by zero-sum to the few established players, +54 pts/day for one farmer. (2) Small real field plus many established low-rated fakes lifts the field-size weight (10 real players + 50 fakes: hero +92 vs +0 without). Beating them is worth nothing, but their headcount raises the weight. Suggested fix (new ALGO_VERSION): count only field members within ~400 of the median rating, or let weight use the real-field count; R9 should decide.
- The simulation's "fakes" check inserts fakes into the same board and compares the farmer's delta with and without them.
