# B2: Replay viewer and ghost race

Files: `packages/client/replay.html`, `src/replay-main.ts`, `src/replay-view.ts`, `vite.config.ts` (second page input), `e2e/replay.spec.ts`.

Use: `/play/replay.html?e=<entryId>` single replay; `&vs=<entryId>` race on one clock; `&embed=1` hides the top bar for iframes.
Fetches `GET /api/entries/:id` and `/replay`; re-runs the log in the main thread (`Timeline`, checkpoints every 250 ticks for scrubbing), draws with the unchanged `FarmView`. A sealed entry (flag or 403) shows "Sealed until the challenge closes". Body data attributes (`data-final-a`, `data-verified-a`, `data-state`) expose the re-run net worth for tests.
Bundle imports only `@tendril/sim` and `farm-view`: no Monaco, no `@tendril/lang` (asserted in the spec, needs a built `dist`/`PW_OUT`).

Checks: `tsc --noEmit` clean; `playwright test replay.spec.ts` 4 passed (verified net worth, race with two canvases, sealed/404 states, bundle contents).
Note: the sandbox has chromium at /opt/pw-browsers/chromium, so the default config's headless shell was not found; ran with executablePath override (not committed).
Requests: B4 should link `/r/<id>` to `/play/replay.html?e=<id>`. A race link needs the other entry id.
Decision: entries of different lengths share one clock of the longer run; the shorter freezes at its end.
