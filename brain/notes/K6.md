- Result: score in the goal's unit, "Provisional until the day closes" with the hidden-farm line; when the entry carries `finalScore` and `provisional=false`: final, visible and hidden totals.
- Practice (`?practice=<seed>`, button "Try another farm"): real brief on another world seed, own save per seed, editor text shared, never submitted (`submitRun` throws), no rating line.
