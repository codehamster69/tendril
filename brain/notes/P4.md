# P4. Drawing budget

Files: new `render-budget.ts` (+ test, added to the client test script), `farm-view.ts`, `farm-light.ts`. Minimal one-line-ish additions: `farm-world.ts` (`rebuilds` counter), `farm-drones.ts` (`moving` getter), `farm-fx.ts` (`active` flag).

## What changed
- **Draw on demand.** The rAF loop stops after 3 quiet frames and restarts on `requestRender()`, an update, input, camera calls, lens/light changes, resize, models arriving. `view.requestRender()`, `view.animating` and `view.holdAnimation()` (returns a release function; exactly once) are the API for other tasks (U2 dial, U3 feedback). Ambient motion (sway, rotors, fireflies, pulses) runs for 2 s after the last update or input, so a running farm stays alive at any speed and a paused one goes fully still. perf meter `frame` hook now fires only on drawn frames, so a paused farm reads 0 fps.
- **Shadows.** `shadowMap.autoUpdate = false`. Redrawn when the sun direction/target, shadow window, farm geometry (world batch rebuild, island, models) change; at most every 100 ms while drones move; once more when they stop. A farm that fits one 36-unit window gets a fixed window centred on the farm, so camera moves never redraw it; bigger farms keep the old following window.
- **Resolution.** Ratio cap 1.5 (Auto), 1 (Low), 2 (High); dynamic: avg frame over 1 s above 20 ms steps down 0.25 to a floor 0.75, below 12 ms steps up. Auto also drops shadows after 2 s still slow at the floor. Antialiasing is set at context creation (Low off, High on, Auto on below 2x DPR), so a quality change applies it on next load; ratio and shadows apply live.
- **Quality key (contract for U5).** `localStorage['tendril.quality']` = `auto` | `low` | `high` (default auto). After writing it, the settings screen should dispatch `window.dispatchEvent(new Event('tendril:quality'))` (a `storage` event from another tab also works).
- **Hidden tab.** Loop and market countdown pause on `visibilitychange`; resume on show.
- **Lite views** (replay/room panes) keep `preserveDrawingBuffer` because the clip exporter copies their canvas outside the render callback; they use Auto without shadows.
- Market countdown is DOM text; it now ticks from its own 1 s interval, not the render loop.

## Not done, and why
- Per-instance incremental updates of world batches (brief item 5): the batches are rebuilt only when the signature changes (update, camera window, lod), never per frame, but one changed plot still rebuilds the visible window's batches. Stable per-tile slots would need a world rewrite; left for a follow-up. Instanced meshes keep `frustumCulled = false` because the view window already limits what is placed.
- Draw-call merging of drone parts (hull, halo, beam, blob, trail per drone, about 6 calls each) would be the next big win; `farm-drones.ts` is outside this task's files.
