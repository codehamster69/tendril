# U12: camera framing beside the slate

- The slate covers the right side of the canvas. `stage.ts` announces the covered width (slate width + 24 px, 0 when hidden) as a `tendril:slate-inset` event. `FarmView` (not lite) passes it to `FarmCamera.setInset`.
- The camera shifts the projection by half the inset (`applyInset`, a view offset), so the frame centres in the free area. The rightmost half-inset is empty and sits under the slate. Ray casts, projections and pointer maps all use the projection, so they stay consistent.
- The inset tweens over `INSET_MS` (250 ms, smoothstep). Reduced motion snaps. When auto-framed, the farm is re-fitted to the free area's aspect.
- `fit` (the existing Jump to farm / Home button) uses the free area. A plot purchase (land grows) re-frames even after the player moved the camera, unless a drone is followed.
- New: `flyTo(target)`, `introGlide()` (on `FarmCamera` and `FarmView`), double-click to centre on the ground under the pointer, and an optional `requestRender` hook called on every view change.
- Decision: key F stays "follow drone" (existing shortcut). Fit is on Home and its button, so the brief's F binding was not added.
