# K2: reference programs and par calibration (2026-11-17, Sonnet)

Contract: `/mnt/project-files/challenges/briefs/00-contract.md`. Sim rules: `docs/SIM_SPEC.md` 6.6. Depends on K5 (#108): the festival program reads `quota()`.

## Files
- `packages/lang/examples/brief-{wealth,festival,specialist,orders,inherited}.sprout` (wealth no longer reuses `daily.sprout`, which walks five tiles and scored a fifth of the others). Each farms every tile, shares tiles across bots (`i % bots() == my_id()`), and only bot 0 spends.
- Shared helpers copied into each file so a player can read one file: `walk(t)` (go_to along the other axis when the first is blocked; bought land from the `land` twist bends, so a plain `go_to` strands the bot), `buy_next_plot()` (tries the four sides of every plot; a refused `buy_plot`/`buy_bot` from a cap just returns false), `spend(reserve)`.
- Festival: grows the unlocked crop with the most quota left, deposits, then sells everything (a deposit counts even if sold later), saves for the cheapest locked crop the fair needs. Specialist: picks the crop with the best `price * yield / ripening time` among unlocked ones and researches a better one when affordable. Orders: grows the crop of the best-paying unlocked order. Inherited: replants wheat on the half-planted farm.
- `packages/lang/scripts/brief-calibrate.ts` (`node --experimental-transform-types --disable-warning=ExperimentalWarning packages/lang/scripts/brief-calibrate.ts daily|season N [goal]`; env `VERBOSE=1`, `FLOORS=n`, `SALT`, `PROG=<example name>`).
- `packages/sim/src/brief.ts`: `MAX_REROLLS`, `BRIEF_FLOOR`, `briefFloor`, `rerollSeed`, `pickBrief`. `packages/server/src/reference.ts`: `REFERENCE_SOURCES`.
- Tests: `packages/lang/test/brief-reference.test.ts` (no runtime errors over 120 dailies, par reachable on 15 dailies + 5 seasons, reroll rule), `packages/server/test/brief-reference.test.ts` (sources identical to the files).

## Calibration (preview salt `calibrate`; the reference scores, mean over visible + hidden farms)
Daily, 600 seeds (about 100 to 150 per goal):
| goal | n | min | p10 | median | p90 | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| wealth | 136 | 320 | 760 | 1616 | 1658 | 3063 |
| inherited | 115 | 1251 | 1950 | 2017 | 2066 | 2495 |
| specialist | 93 | 320 | 994 | 2819 | 6399 | 22360 |
| orders | 109 | 320 | 422 | 536 | 729 | 1334 |
| festival (permille) | 147 | 0 | 76 | 545 | 909 | 1000 |
Seasons, 30 seeds (5 farms), medians: wealth 7171, inherited 6680, specialist 18081, orders 1678, festival 850 permille.
- **Impossible:** only festival. Pools of clover, pumpkin and berry alone score 0 to 90 permille (650 coins of research from 30 coins). 23 of 147 festival draws (16%) are under the 250 floor; the other goals are under 2% (wealth 3 extra plots with one bot; orders with 3 extra plots). With 9 tries the chance that all fail is negligible.
- **Trivial:** `daily.sprout` (the first program a player writes) reaches 90% of the reference mean in 1 of 93 specialist and 1 of 136 wealth days, 0 elsewhere. The bundled `expander` and `orchard` examples score lower than the references on every goal, so the references are not weak.
- **Hidden farms:** festival and inherited score the same on hidden and visible farms (deposits and replanting do not depend on soil or weather); orders differ more than 2x on about 9% of days (order draws). Hidden farms mostly matter for wealth, specialist and orders.
- **Tuning:** no change to `QUOTA_UNIT` or twist strengths. Goals are not comparable in absolute units, only within a day. The festival problem is the draw (all-expensive pools), which the floor handles; if Rohan wants fewer festival rerolls, K1 can force the pool to contain carrot or wheat (not done: it removes a real planning problem).
- Floors (SIM_SPEC 6.6) are about the 2nd to 5th percentile of each goal's reference score.

## For K3
Run the reference through verify with the stored brief seed; for `pickBrief` pass the visible farm's score (`replayWithGoal(...).score` of `REFERENCE_SOURCES[brief.goal.kind]`). Par = max(1, floor(mean score over visible + hidden farms * 90 / 100)). Hidden world seeds come from the challenge id, not the rerolled seed. A verify call is about 0.2 s for a daily reference; a creation costs at most 9 x 1 visible farm plus 2 (daily) or 4 (season) hidden farms.
